Résumé templates

The four approved designs for Resume Tailor, with Meridian as the default. Each one is a matching set: a résumé and a cover letter that share the same letterhead, fonts, and accent color. They all use the same sample data, a Senior Platform Engineer applying for a Staff role, so you can compare the designs directly. Click a page to open it full size. From there, Print / Save as PDF shows how the exported file will look.

US Letter · 8.5 × 11 in Real, selectable text (ATS-parseable) Accent and fonts are theme options Mockup fonts load from Google Fonts; the app bundles them

Meridian

Default ATS: Excellent

Modern and crisp single column. A navy accent bar runs across the top. The name is large, contact details have small icons, section headings are small caps with a hairline rule, and dates are right-aligned. Works for most roles.

Atlas

ATS: Good

The most eye-catching option. A tinted sidebar holds a monogram, contact details, and skills as chips. The main column shows experience as a timeline. The cover letter's sidebar has a "Highlights" panel with the candidate's numbers. In the page markup, the main column comes first, so parsers read the experience before the sidebar.

ATS: Excellent

Classic and editorial. A letter-spaced Garamond name, a centered header, burgundy double rules, and serif body text. Built for finance, legal, government, academic, and executive roles.
