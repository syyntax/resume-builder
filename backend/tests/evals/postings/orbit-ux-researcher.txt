UX Researcher
Orbit Mobility — San Francisco, CA or remote

Orbit runs bike and scooter sharing in 30 cities. Our research team helps product teams understand riders and city partners.

What you'll do
- Plan and run qualitative and quantitative research: interviews, diary studies, usability tests, and surveys
- Synthesize findings into clear recommendations and share them across the company
- Partner with designers and product managers to shape the roadmap
- Build research operations: participant recruiting, a research repository, and templates

What we're looking for
- 4+ years of UX research experience
- Strong command of both qualitative and quantitative methods, including survey design and analysis
- Experience with statistics tools (R, SPSS, or Python) is a plus
- Excellent storytelling and presentation skills

Nice to have
- Experience in transportation or marketplaces
- Field research experience
